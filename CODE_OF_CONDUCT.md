# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and professional experience for everyone, regardless of level of experience, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviour includes:

- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback
- Being direct and honest about tradeoffs and limitations
- Staying focused on the technical work
- Helping others understand the codebase

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Personal attacks or harassment of any kind
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of technical discussions

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and addressed appropriately. Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.
